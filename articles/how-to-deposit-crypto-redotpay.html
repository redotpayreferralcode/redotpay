Method 1: Direct On-Chain Crypto Transfer (0% RedotPay Fee)

  1. Open the RedotPay App and tap "Deposit" on the homepage.
  2. Select the coin you wish to transfer (e.g. USDT).
  3. Select your preferred blockchain network (BSC BEP20 or TRC20 recommended for fees under $1).
  4. Copy your unique RedotPay deposit address or scan the QR code from your exchange/wallet (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Trust Wallet, MetaMask).
  5. Send the crypto and wait for blockchain block confirmations (typically 1–3 minutes).
