Common Reasons for KYC Failure and How to Fix Them

1. Glare or Cut-Off Corners

Ensure all four corners of your ID document are clearly visible in the camera frame without reflective flash glare obscuring your birthdate or document number.

2. Name Mismatch

The name you typed during initial registration must match the exact spelling and order on your government ID document.

3. Restricted Country IP/Document

Submitting IDs from unsupported jurisdictions (e.g. United States, Mainland China, Russia) will result in immediate registration disqualification.
