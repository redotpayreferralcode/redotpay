Why Connect RedotPay to Apple Pay & Google Wallet?

Adding your card to your mobile wallet transforms your cryptocurrency into a tap-to-pay mechanism everywhere contactless Visa payments are accepted:

  • Instant Virtual Card Use: No need to wait for physical plastic delivery—spend right away on your iPhone, Apple Watch, or Android device.
  • Enhanced Biometric Security: Transactions require Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint passcode, protecting your balance.
  • Dynamic Tokenization: Your actual card numbers are never exposed to point-of-sale merchant terminals.

How to Add RedotPay Card to Apple Pay (iPhone & Apple Watch)

  1. Open the RedotPay App and navigate to the Cards tab.
  2. Tap on your active Virtual or Physical card and tap "Card Details" to reveal the 16-digit card number, expiration date, and CVV.
  3. Open the Apple Wallet app on your iPhone.
  4. Tap the "+" icon in the top-right corner.
  5. Select "Debit or Credit Card" > "Enter Card Details Manually".
  6. Enter your name, 16-digit card number, expiration date, and security code (CVV).
  7. Accept the Visa terms and complete the verification via SMS OTP sent to your registered mobile phone number.

How to Add RedotPay Card to Google Pay / Google Wallet (Android)

  1. Open the Google Wallet app on your Android device.
  2. Tap "Add to Wallet" at the bottom.
  3. Choose "Payment card" > "New credit or debit card".
  4. Enter your 16-digit RedotPay card credentials from the RedotPay app.
  5. Confirm your billing address and submit.
  6. Verify via SMS one-time passcode to activate tap-to-pay immediately.

Troubleshooting Common Mobile Wallet Errors

"Card Not Added / Contact Issuer"

Ensure your card is activated and has at least $1–$2 in crypto balance for the temporary $0.00–$1.00 pre-authorization ping.

SMS Code Not Arriving

Check that your international country code is correctly registered in RedotPay profile settings.
