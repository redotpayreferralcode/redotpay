Which RedotPay Card Supports ATM Withdrawals?

Only the Physical Card ($100 USD) supports cash withdrawals at physical ATM machines. The Virtual Card cannot be used at traditional cash dispensers.

How to Set or Change Your ATM PIN

  1. Open the RedotPay App and tap on the Cards tab.
  2. Select your Physical Card.
  3. Tap "Security Settings" > "Card PIN".
  4. Enter your desired 6-digit or 4-digit PIN and confirm with biometric authentication.

Golden Rule: Always Decline ATM Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC)

When an international ATM asks: "Would you like this ATM to convert currency for you at a guaranteed rate?", ALWAYS SELECT NO / DEBIT IN LOCAL CURRENCY. Letting the ATM bank do the conversion can add a 5%–10% markup, whereas RedotPay's exchange rate is only 1.2%.
